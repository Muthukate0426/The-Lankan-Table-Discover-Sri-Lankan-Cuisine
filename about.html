About The Lankan Table

Sharing the Taste of Sri Lanka

The Lankan Table is a website dedicated to introducing authentic Sri Lankan cuisine to food lovers around the world.

Our mission is to preserve traditional recipes and share Sri Lanka's rich culinary heritage with everyone.

From Rice & Curry to Kottu Roti, Hoppers, Pittu, Lamprais, and delicious desserts, we celebrate every flavor of Sri Lankan food.
